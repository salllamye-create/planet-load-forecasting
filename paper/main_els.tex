\title{Closed-Form Calendar Estimation in Deep Load Forecasting: An Information-Controlled Benchmark on Eight National Grids}

\author[a]{Abdulwahed Salam\corref{cor1}}
\ead{salllam.ye@gmail.com}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author.}
\affiliation[a]{organization={Independent researcher}, country={Yemen}}

\begin{abstract}
Electricity load is the aggregate of human schedules on a shared calendar, yet deep
forecasters learn its periodicity from the lookback window. PLANET adds to a deep backbone a
calendar tier: a harmonic design matrix with weekend and public-holiday day-types whose
coefficients are re-estimated per window in closed form inside the forward pass, followed by
a residual network and a covariate head shared by every model in the study. Removing the tier
raises the error of two unrelated backbones by \nv{abl.patch.no_pl.mean_dwape_pct} and
\nv{abl.basis.no_pl.mean_dwape_pct}, most on the German system and least on the
French one; locking the basis to the calendar rather than the window is worth
\nv{abl.patch.relative.mean_dwape_pct} and \nv{abl.basis.relative.mean_dwape_pct} on the two
backbones, and the choice of local time over UTC nothing measurable. On an information-set-controlled benchmark of eight national systems with weather
at forecast skill, holidays and the operators' published day-ahead forecasts,
\nv{n.models.learned} learned forecasters and two zero-shot foundation models receive
identical inputs under one tuning budget, three seeds and three information regimes, and are
compared with model confidence sets. PLANET is the second most accurate point and
probabilistic forecaster, retained in \nv{mcs.grid.PLANET.n_in} of \nv{mcs.grid.n_cells}
confidence sets; the covariate-native TiDE is the most accurate, and a control that feeds
TiDE through the shared head measures how much of its margin the covariate pathway accounts
for. Against the learned-calendar control, the closed-form tier gains most with a short
history and under transfer between systems. The seed ensemble of the two leading models is ahead of the operators' forecast on
\nv{da.grid.Comb2-ens.n_beats_tso_unbiased} of the six systems whose published series is
unbiased, significantly on \nv{da.grid.Comb2-ens.n_sig_beats_tso_unbiased}. Giving a fixed architecture the calendar and the weather changes
its error by \nv{info.grid.R2_vs_R0.mean_dwape_pct}, more than the gap between the proposed model and the median learned method.
\end{abstract}
